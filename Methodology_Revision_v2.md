# Video-first database v2 — methodology revision

## Decision
The unit of source analysis is one complete available video transcript. A video can support several subject categories without being split into unrelated source fragments. Categories and research questions are provisional; chapters are built only after contextual consolidation.

## What changed
Whole-video purpose and format precede subject mapping. Primary, supporting and context-only contributions are distinct. A mention does not create a chapter. Course introductions and numbered excerpts are not automatically complete courses. Promotional sources can contain teaching, but claims about mentoring outcomes remain promotional. Market-update observations remain situated examples unless the source generalizes them.

## Delivered state
695 source rows, 695 intact timestamped HTML transcripts and 695 ordered JSON source records preserve all 320,407 original segments. Thirteen complete available transcripts were read for this redesign and received contextual assessments. The remaining 682 await whole-video review. Earlier 63 evidence occurrences remain available separately and do not count as whole-video review completion. The original master index is retained unchanged.

## Workflow
Read each available transcript end to end; determine format, purpose and argument; identify primary/supporting subjects; link only developed contributions to research questions; preserve illustrative versus generalized scope; check speaker and chart dependence; consolidate subject answers; revisit whole sources where exceptions or conflicts arise. Audit coverage and representative mappings before course construction.

## Completion gate replacing the old block gate
Every video must have a whole-video assessment or documented reason it cannot be assessed. Every material contribution must be traceable to the complete source and timestamp. Consolidated answers require a question-level adequacy review: definitions, conditions, exceptions, examples and source disagreements. Audit random unmapped sources and incidental mentions for missed substantive contributions. Check attribution, caption gaps and duplicate source families. Repeat targeted retrieval where open questions remain. Do not claim exhaustive knowledge or treat a fixed number of blocks as proof of semantic completeness.

## Current verdict
DATABASE RESTRUCTURE: COMPLETE, subject to delivered verification. KNOWLEDGE CONSOLIDATION: PARTIAL. COURSE SYNTHESIS: NOT READY. The prior Phase 2C block-completeness gate is superseded by this video-first protocol; its failures and source checks are retained, not erased. No Phase 2D course claim database is certified by this redesign.

## Reading the package
Extract the entire ZIP before opening Video_First_Database_v2.xlsx; keep transcripts beside the workbook. Its complete-transcript hyperlinks use relative paths. Open index.html for a searchable transcript collection. The workbook alone does not contain the external transcript files. HTML files include every source segment; JSON files preserve the original ordered records. Upload dates are not recording dates. Captions can be a complete available source while the uploaded video itself is an excerpt.

## Provenance and uncertainty
Speaker evidence is inherited from the conservative transcript audit, not newly verified identity. Thirteen new assessments are single-pass text reviews; visuals are not reviewed. Two provisional duplicate pairs retain separate upload IDs and are barred from independent corroboration counts until resolved. Caption-tail flags are preserved from the prior mechanical audit. No outside trading sources were used. The question framework is analyst-authored, not a statement of what every speaker teaches.
